Privacy Policy

OnlyBobs / MangaTracker is a private, personal-use application used only by its owner.

What data is accessed

When the owner logs in with TikTok, the app reads only the owner's own public profile information (display name, avatar), profile statistics (follower, following and like counts) and the list of the owner's own public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.

How it is stored

This data is stored in the owner's own private database on the owner's own server. Access tokens are stored encrypted. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Deletion

The owner can disconnect the account inside the app at any time, which removes the stored login tokens, and can revoke the app's access in TikTok's settings. Stored statistics can be deleted on request by the owner of the app.
